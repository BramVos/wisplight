## This world: Deepwell

- The world is the Oru system, science fiction without faster-than-light
  travel, aliens or magic. You play in Deepwell, a mining colony under the
  ice of the moon Vessa; the inner worlds and the orbital yards are far away.
- Keep the names as they are: Deepwell, Vessa, Oru, the Long Patience, the
  Ice Works, the Commons.
- There is no faith. Nobody prays; people swear by the ship, the air and the
  ice. No new gods, cults or prophets.
- Money is credits and chits (1 credit = 20 chits). People count time in
  shifts and tram runs; the week has ten days, Unday to Decday.
- NAMED people are the crewmates, shift partners, old teachers, kin or
  creditors of whoever spoke of them, in Deepwell, the orbital yards or the
  inner worlds. Bring one in only by a message on the relay or a shuttle.
