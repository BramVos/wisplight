######## THE PLAYER ACTS
The shuttle hatch sighs open, and the cold comes in before the light does. You follow the other passengers down a ribbed gangway into an airlock that smells of metal and old air.

Somewhere beyond the inner door a tram hums along its rail, and a voice on a speaker reads out a list of shift numbers nobody listens to.

This is Deepwell, under the ice of Vessa. You came here for work, and nobody has told you where to find it yet.

Type LOOK to look around. Type HELP if you are lost.
The Shuttle Airlock
You stand in a ribbed steel airlock big enough for a cargo sled, its walls furred with frost where the outer door meets the frame. The air smells of metal and of other people's breath, used many times. A faded poster of the Long Patience is taped beside the inner door. The inner door opens east onto the Commons.
Exits: east
The pace of events is normal. Type TEMPO CALM or TEMPO DRAMATIC for less or more happening in the world.

> down
The Dome Platform
A narrow platform runs beside a single rail in a tunnel cut through the ice. When the tram is coming, the rail hums before you can see anything. A timetable is painted on the wall in yellow. The stair goes up to the Commons; a service tunnel runs east beside the rail, the long way to the Ice Works on foot.
Exits: up, east

> take the tram
You tap your card for 3 ch, and the tram slides off along its rail. After 25 minutes it stops at The Works Platform.

> look
The Works Platform
The rail ends here against a buffer of packed ice, and the platform opens into a hall full of sleds and hoses. Your breath smokes, and the cold gets in through the seams of your clothes. A board lists the day's cut in tonnes. The cutting face is further in; the service tunnel runs back west to the Domes.
Exits: in, west
