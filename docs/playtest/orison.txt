######## THE PLAYER ACTS
The Quiet Reach
The supply ship settles onto the pad with a long shudder, and the engines die into the sound of rain on the hull. Through the port you see low cloud, black rock and a grey sea that never seems to end. This is Nacre, at the far edge of the Lantern Belt, months from Harrow Station, where you signed the contract.

Your assignment sounds modest. Up on Orison Ridge an abandoned listening station has been catching a repeating signal from somewhere no human has ever been, and now the station is failing. Someone in Port Vesper wants the recordings recovered, the fault found and the data checked for tampering. Not everyone here, you suspect, wants the same answer.

The outer door of the Arrival Lock seals behind you. Pumps thud, the air turns cold and smells of salt and machine oil, and the inner door begins to open.

Type LOOK to look around. Type HELP if you are lost.

You keep old machines alive for a living, and the listening station on Orison Ridge is failing for reasons nobody here can name. Dr Sorell sent for you to find out why before the original recordings are lost.
You were told to ask for the research lead in Port Vesper. Why you are here, and the name, are in your journal (J).
Arrival Lock
Steel walls drip from the pumps, and seawater pools in the grooves of the floor grating. Crates from the supply ship line one wall, each label stamped [Harrow Station]. The cold tastes of salt and oil; a pressure pump thuds behind the panels. By the inner door, a desk scanner reads assignments and your terminal chirps, taking the local map. A corridor leads east, a cargo passage northeast.
Exits: east, northeast
Here: the port coordinator (at work).
Arrival Lock
The inner door of the lock opens on steel, seawater and the thud of pumps, and for the first time in months the floor under you does not move. Every crate against the wall came the same long way you did, from Harrow Station.
The pace of events is normal: TEMPO CALM or TEMPO DRAMATIC changes it.
New quest: The Orison Recordings. Dr Sorell wants the original recordings from Orison Ridge. Niko Serrin found the anomalous transmission, and he knows the station better than anyone.

Orison Listening Room
Cramped and lined with receiving consoles, half dark and the rest flickering fault codes, this room hums with a rack of recording drives beside a desk where the maintenance logs lie in a damp-swollen binder. Through the wide window the antenna rises into cloud, cables groaning. A speaker gives the same short pattern of tones, then silence. Stairs lead down the steep route to [Ridge Shelter].
Exits: down
